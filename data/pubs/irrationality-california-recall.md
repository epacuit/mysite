abstract: We analyze California’s gubernatorial recall system using basic ideas
  from the mathematical theory of social choice. We expose some pathologies of
  the system, use computer simulations to explore how often these pathologies
  might occur, investigate the effects of strategic voting on the analysis, and
  suggest changes to the system that cure the pathologies.
